How to read this

  • Duration is active time: every gap between two events of a run is capped at the --gap threshold, so sleep, permission waits and left-open sessions do not inflate it. The wait between a Bash call and its result counts up to 2 hours, since that is the command running.
  • Median / P90 / P99: half / nine in ten / ninety-nine in a hundred runs are shorter. Watch P90 and the weekly lines, not the median: long runs live in the tail.
  • Interrupts are the "[Request interrupted by user]" markers inside a run. Stepping in when you see a problem is a choice about where your attention goes, not a score to minimise; compare it with how long runs last between interruptions.
  • runner = at least 5 runs and at least 30% of them over 10 minutes. new = first seen in the last 14 days of the data.
  • A run ends at your next prompt, so one long piece of work you steered with "continue" shows up as several runs. Subagent work is part of its parent's run.
